# Yarn Storage 

## Problem Statement

It's estimated that over 50 million people worldwide crochet or knit, with knitting dating back 1,000 years. All of these people must use the most important thing for their projects - yarn. Most crafters have a significant amount, whether they bought it for a project they haven't done yet, they have leftovers from a project, or they simply bought it because they liked the look of it. It tends to pile up, literally. Crafters have found a need for a system that can keep track of this yarn, know how much they have on hand and what did they plan to do with it. Currently, you could keep a log on a spreadsheet or in a notebook. This can be hard to search, especially when on paper. This is hard to keep track of projects you had planned for the yarn and gets cluttered and chaotic looking. Some people have even resorted to using 3D printers to print the information on small hooks you can attach to projects, which is just not a sustainable method both timewise and reuse. This application organizes the yarn with all it's information, allow you to search for projects with yarn you don't have plans for, and keep track of the projects you already have started but just haven't had time to finish, without guessing what hook you need to use or where you need to go to buy more.
