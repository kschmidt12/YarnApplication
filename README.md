# Katrina Schmidt Individual Project

## Problem Statement

It's estimated that over 50 million people worldwide crochet or knit, with knitting dating back 1,000 years. All of these people must use the most important thing for their projects - yarn. Most crafters have a significant amount, whether they bought it for a project they haven't done yet, they have leftovers from a project, or they simply bought it because they liked the look of it. It tends to pile up, literally. Crafters have found a need for a system that can keep track of this yarn, know how much they have on hand and what did they plan to do with it. Possibly look for ideas of what to do with it. This application will help do just that and help them find ideas for all the yarn they have hidden away.
